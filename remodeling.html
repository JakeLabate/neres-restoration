Kitchen and bathroom remodeling

The same family owned crew that repairs the outside of a building remodels the rooms inside it. Kitchens, bathrooms, tile, plaster and drywall, for residential and commercial property across Greater Boston, with same day appointments available.

The anatomy of the work

What a bathroom hides behind the tile

The visible remodel is the last quarter inch. The rest of the wall is the part that decides whether it lasts.

Kitchen remodeling

We remodel kitchens, from a single wall to the whole room. The usual goal is a kitchen that is more functional to work in and more current to look at, and those two things do not always want the same layout, which is why the plan starts with how you actually cook rather than with a picture.

Call for a same day appointment and we will walk the room with you. The estimate is free.

Bathroom remodeling

We remodel bathrooms, including the parts nobody sees: the substrate under the tile, the waterproofing behind the wet wall, the slope of the pan. A bathroom that looks new and leaks is a worse outcome than the one you started with, so the invisible work is where the job is won.

Tile replacement

We replace broken and stained tile in bathrooms and kitchens. Tile is often the cheapest thing in a room to fix and the first thing anyone looks at, and when the tile is the whole problem, replacing it is the whole job. Cracked grout and hollow sounding tiles are worth a call before water gets under them.

Plaster and drywall

We handle plaster and drywall, which is what most masonry and remodeling jobs eventually turn into once a wall is open. Patches finished to match the surface around them, not just filled.

General contracting

We work as general contractors on residential and commercial projects, which means one number to call when a job crosses from the masonry into the rooms, and one crew accountable for how the whole thing lands on your vision, your needs and your budget.
