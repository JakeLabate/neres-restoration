Masonry restoration

We maintain, restore and tuckpoint brick, stone and block on residential and commercial property across the Greater Boston area. This is the trade the rest of the business grew out of, and it has been the core of it since 2005.

The anatomy of the repair

One joint, before and after

Most masonry failure is mortar failure, and most mortar failure looks like the upper joint below. The repair is the lower one.

Repointing and tuckpointing

Mortar fails before brick does. Joints crack and recede from the face, water gets behind the wall, and the freeze and thaw cycle widens every gap it finds over a winter or two. Open joints, crumbling mortar you can rake out with a finger, and white mineral staining are all the same problem announcing itself.

We rake the failed mortar out to sound depth, repack the joint with mortar matched to the wall, and tool it flush. The match matters: a hard modern mix forced into a wall of softer brick transfers the stress to the brick itself, which is how a cheap repointing job turns into spalled faces a few years later. Tuckpointing is the core of what we do, and on most walls it is the difference between a repair and a rebuild.

Brick and stone repair

We repair damaged brickwork on residential and commercial property, and rebuild sections that are past repointing: bulged panels, shifted corners, brick that has lost its face. Where you want something added rather than fixed, we build new brick and stone to sit with what is already there rather than against it.

Chimneys and fireplaces

A chimney is the most exposed masonry on a house. It takes weather from four sides and heat from inside, so it is usually the first thing to need attention and the easiest to catch early, while the fix is still a crown and some joints rather than a rebuild from the roofline.

We repair chimneys, we repair and construct brick and stone fireplaces, and we handle fireplace cleaning.

Foundations and structures

We repair and construct foundations and structural masonry. Foundation work runs from repointing a fieldstone wall that has started letting water into a basement to rebuilding a failed section outright. Water finds the foundation last, which means by the time it shows up inside, the joint that let it in has usually been open for a while.

Steps, stairs and stucco

Masonry stairs take more weather, more salt and more impact than any other part of a property, and they fail at the tread and the nose first. We rebuild and repair masonry steps and stairs so they are solid underfoot again, and we repair and finish stucco walls.

Gutters

Gutters are on the list for a reason: a failed gutter dumps roof water straight down a wall, and that wall's mortar joints pay for it. Handling the gutter and the masonry as one problem beats fixing the wall and leaving the cause in place.

Building restoration

Beyond single defects, we take on building restoration: the brick walls, the envelope and the masonry a building depends on, for residential and commercial buildings throughout the Greater Boston area, with the craftsmanship, follow ups and personalized attention the work deserves.
