Driveways, patios and walls

Hardscaping and exteriors

We design and install the stone and brick that sits outside the building: driveways, walkways, patios, retaining walls and fencing, across the Greater Boston area. Custom design comes first, because hardscape that ignores the site does not stay flat.

The anatomy of the work

Most of a patio is under the patio

The surface is what you see. The layers below it are what you are actually paying for, because they are what keep it flat through the winters.

Stone driveways and walkways

We custom design and install stone driveways and walkways. The design comes before the first stone goes down, because a walkway that ignores how water moves across the site heaves in the first hard winter, and a driveway laid over a poor base ruts under the first season of cars.

Get the grade, the base and the drainage right and the surface is the easy part. That is the order we work in.

Brick and stone patios

We build patios in brick and stone, sized and laid out for how the space is actually used rather than for how it looks in a catalog. A patio is a floor that lives outside, so it gets the same base preparation a driveway does, scaled to what will stand on it.

Retaining walls and hardscape design

We finish an exterior with the installation of a retaining wall or a hardscape design suited to the grade of the site. On a sloped lot the retaining wall is the piece everything else depends on: it is holding ground, not decorating it, and it has to be built to drain or the ground it holds will eventually push it over.

Replacing decks and wooden fences

Wooden decks and fences age badly in this climate. They rot at the posts, loosen at the fasteners, and demand refinishing on a schedule most people abandon after the second round. We replace and remodel aging wood with durable patios and brick fencing: the same footprint, in a material that asks for almost nothing afterward.

Brick walls

We build and repair brick walls, freestanding and structural, for residential and commercial property. A garden wall and a load bearing wall are different jobs that happen to look alike, and we build each one as what it is.
