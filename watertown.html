Serving Watertown from Watertown

Masonry and remodeling in Watertown

The home base. Neres Restoration has been based in Watertown since 2005, so the walls, chimneys and squares here are the ones we drive past every day.

Where the trucks start from

Watertown built out in the streetcar era, and it shows in the stock: two families and triple deckers off Mount Auburn Street and Arsenal Street, brick commercial blocks in the squares, and a century of chimneys, foundations and front steps that have all taken the same hundred New England winters. Mortar of that age is at the stage of life where repointing is routine maintenance, not an emergency.

What we bring to Watertown

The full trade, from one family owned crew: masonry restoration including repointing, chimneys, foundations and steps; hardscaping from walkways and patios to retaining walls and brick fencing; and kitchen and bathroom remodeling, tile, plaster and drywall.

Serving Watertown as part of the Greater Boston area since 2005, for residential and commercial property. Our professionals speak Portuguese and Spanish along with English, hours run 8am to 8pm seven days a week, and 24/7 emergency work is available.

The practical part
